Read the boss asset brief and current v3 encounter card before judging art.

A boss reference fails if it cannot communicate:
- attack source,
- anticipation,
- active threat,
- recovery,
- phase change,
- weak point/objective,
- collision/readability at gameplay camera distance.

A new phase must not be only color/VFX.
Never let obsolete encounter art overrule current mechanics.
Face-Eater v3 rules are a critical conflict check.
